# About this data

`practice_anova.csv` — real CS111 midterm data.

- `group` — whether the student practised old-exam papers before the exam:
  `practice` (did) or `non_practice` (did not).
- `midterm_score` — total midterm score, out of 400.

**Anonymised.** Student IDs were dropped at extraction — not hashed, not
pseudonymised, simply not included. Rows are sorted, so row order carries no
information either. Withdrawn and absent students are not in the file.

Two columns. That is everything in it.
