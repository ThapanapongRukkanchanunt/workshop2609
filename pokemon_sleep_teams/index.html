How these numbers are made

  • A team "covers" a recipe when its average ingredients per day, divided by 3 meals, reach every amount in the recipe at max pot size. Surplus shows what's left over per meal.
  • Each Pokémon uses an ingredient-focused build: Quiet nature, Ingredient Finder M, Helping Speed M and Ingredient Finder S (as the level allows), max main skill and max sleep ribbon. Sleep 21:30–06:00, no Good Camp Ticket, no cooking-skill bonuses beyond what support Pokémon add.
  • Bonus % is the recipe's strength bonus over the raw value of its ingredients (before recipe level and Good Camp/event bonuses).
  • Ingredients listed per Pokémon are its slots at Lv 1 / 30 / 60. Only slots unlocked at the chosen level are shown.
  • The minimum team size is exact: every combination up to 5 Pokémon was checked against the solver's per-Pokémon production, which also caught smaller teams the solver's own search had missed. Teams are listed with the most slack first. Real teams with weaker natures or subskills produce less.
