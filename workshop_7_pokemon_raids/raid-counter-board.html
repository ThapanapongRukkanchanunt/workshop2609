Method, caveats & sources

Ordering follows Pokémon GO Hub's Score (a blend of DPS and TDO), cross-checked against Pokebattler's estimator-based points. Where the two disagree it is called out in the note on the row.

Where scores sit close together, the pick that actually gets STAB on the type is preferred over a generalist running off-type coverage.

"Legendary" here means the raid-exclusive pool: Legendary, Mythical and Ultra Beast. Kartana, Xurkitree, Blacephalon, Nihilego, Pheromosa, Naganadel, Guzzlord and Stakataka sit in Legendary slots because you cannot get them as normal spawns.

Move notation follows the sources: * = legacy or exclusive move, + = upgraded move variant.

Availability: Shadow Legendaries rotate through Giovanni and Shadow Raids, so several entries are "best on paper" rather than catchable this week. Amber rows are either unreleased, rotated out, or genuinely not worth building — read the note before spending candy.
