# Pokémon GO — Best Raid Attackers by Attack Type

Compiled 18 September 2026 from [Pokémon GO Hub's DB](https://db.pokemongohub.net/best/attackers-per-type)
(per-type DPS/TDO/Score tables) and [Pokebattler's raid attacker rankings](https://www.pokebattler.com/raids/attackers/rankings).

For every attacking type there are **10 picks**:

| Slot | Count |
|---|---|
| Mega / Primal | 2 |
| Shadow Legendary | 1 |
| Shadow non-Legendary | 2 |
| Legendary (non-Shadow) | 3 |
| Non-Legendary, non-Shadow | 2 |

## How to read this

- **"Legendary"** here means the raid-exclusive pool: Legendary, Mythical **and** Ultra Beast.
  Ultra Beasts (Kartana, Xurkitree, Blacephalon, Nihilego, Pheromosa, Naganadel, Guzzlord,
  Stakataka) sit in the Legendary slots because you cannot get them as normal spawns.
- Ordering follows Pokémon GO Hub's **Score** (a blend of DPS and TDO), cross-checked against
  Pokebattler's estimator-based points. Where the two disagree it is called out in the notes.
- Where scores are close, the pick that actually gets **STAB** on the type is preferred over a
  generalist running off-type coverage.
- Move notation follows the sources: `*` = legacy / exclusive move, `+` = upgraded move variant.
- **Availability caveat:** Shadow Legendaries rotate through Giovanni and Shadow Raids, so several
  entries below are "best on paper" rather than "catchable this week". Rows marked ⚠️ are either
  unreleased/rotated-out or genuinely not worth building — read the note before you spend candy.

## Quick takeaways

- **If you build one thing per type:** Mega Rayquaza (Flying/Dragon), Mega Mewtwo X (Fighting),
  Mega Mewtwo Y (Psychic), Primal Groudon (Ground), Primal Kyogre (Water),
  Crowned Sword Zacian (Steel) and Mega Diancie (Rock) cover the widest span of raid bosses.
- **Best value non-Legendary shadows:** Shadow Metagross, Shadow Rhyperior, Shadow Garchomp,
  Shadow Mamoswine, Shadow Tyranitar — all are cheap, bulky, and top-5 in their type.
- **Types with no real Shadow Legendary option:** Bug, Fairy, Grass and Rock. The entries there are
  technical bests, not recommendations.
- Mega/Primal picks also boost the whole lobby's damage for their type, so bringing one is worth
  more than its own DPS suggests.
